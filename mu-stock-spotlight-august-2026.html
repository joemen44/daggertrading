MU: Where Does Micron Find Support After the Sharp Pullback?

Published August 19, 2026

30-Second Summary

What matters most

  • MU has shifted from a strong short-term run into a sharp pullback, with shorter-timeframe momentum weakening.
  • $932.64 is the immediate level I'm watching after playing an important role Tuesday and into extended hours.
  • If MU cannot reclaim and hold that area, $921–$917 becomes increasingly important.
  • If that zone fails, $908–$910 stands out as deeper potential support with larger Fibonacci confluence.
  • The goal isn't to predict the bottom. It's to watch how price reacts as these levels are tested.

Why MU Sold Off Tuesday

MU closed Tuesday at $941.10, down 7%, abruptly ending a five-day winning streak that had pushed the stock above $1,000 on Monday. The weakness wasn't isolated to Micron. Semiconductor, memory and optical stocks sold off broadly, with Sandisk down 9% and SK Hynix ADRs down 9.2%, while several other AI-related chip names also finished sharply lower.

One of the major concerns was rising Treasury yields. Higher yields can increase financing costs across the economy, and for Micron the bigger question isn't necessarily its own debt load. Barron's noted that Micron had approximately $5.72 billion in total debt as of May. The concern is whether higher funding costs could eventually pressure the enormous amount of AI-infrastructure spending being planned by Micron's customers.

MarketWatch also reported that investors were weighing very high expectations across the AI trade, including new financial figures surrounding AI startup Anthropic. Analysts noted that the numbers were impressive but may have fallen short of increasingly aggressive expectations. That added another reason for investors to take profits after the recent run in AI hardware names.

Strong fundamentals. Weakening momentum. Important levels ahead.

The selloff comes after an extraordinary run. Barron's reported that MU had gained more than 700% over the previous 12 months. When a stock has moved that far and expectations are extremely high, profit-taking and sharp volatility can become part of the picture even when the longer-term fundamental story remains strong.

There is still an important counterpoint. Analysts cited in the reports noted that demand for memory and storage products tied to AI remains strong. Micron and Sandisk have also been striking longer-term customer agreements that may provide more pricing stability than in previous memory cycles. Barron's reported that Wall Street analysts had an average Micron price target of $1,566, according to FactSet.

That creates an interesting conflict for MU right now: the longer-term AI and memory story remains compelling, while short-term technical momentum has weakened considerably. That's why I'm focusing on price reaction around the levels below rather than assuming either the bulls or bears have already won.

The $932.64 level matters

One of the most important short-term levels I'm watching is $932.64. This is a 200% Pivot Point Fibonacci extension that played an important role during Tuesday's trading and continued drawing reactions into extended hours.

I've noticed an interesting behavior with these Pivot Point Fibonacci extensions over time: when price loses the 200% extension and that level begins acting as resistance, price will often work its way back toward the 100% level. If the 100% level subsequently becomes resistance as well, the earlier 61.8%–50% Fibonacci area can eventually come back into play.

Important: This is not a rule or prediction. It's a recurring price behavior I've observed and something I'm watching closely with MU.

A reclaim of $932.64 followed by a successful hold as support would be an encouraging first step. Continued rejection underneath it keeps the lower levels in focus.

$921–$917 is the next important support zone

The next area that stands out is approximately $921.93–$917.64. This zone has significance within the previous Fibonacci structure, including the earlier 61.8%/50% support area, and overlaps with other nearby technical references.

That makes it more useful as a support zone than an exact price where MU must reverse. If buyers establish themselves here, MU could attempt a short-term bounce. If the area fails, the next zone becomes considerably more interesting.

$908–$910 could be a bigger test

The $909.65 area stands out on the larger chart as the 38.2% Fibonacci retracement from a previous major swing low to swing high. Another technical level sits near $908, creating additional confluence.

That makes approximately $908–$910 one of the more interesting potential support areas I'm watching if MU continues lower. Reaching a Fibonacci level does not automatically mean the stock will bounce. I'd still want to see buyers respond, momentum stabilize, and price confirm that the area is being respected.

The August 13–17 move adds another clue

I also drew a separate Fibonacci retracement from the August 13 swing low to the August 17 swing high to measure how much of MU's latest run is being retraced.

The shorter-term Fib helps put the current pullback in context while the older Fibonacci structure identifies larger support areas. When levels from separate swings begin clustering in the same area, I pay more attention to the zone—but the reaction still matters more than the line itself.

Momentum has weakened

The shorter timeframes have clearly deteriorated. On the 1-hour chart, MU is trading underneath its short-term exponential moving averages, creating a bearish short-term structure. The 8-hour MACD has also rolled bearish following the recent bounce.

The daily picture is more nuanced. MU remains well above its major longer-term moving averages, so I would not characterize the entire larger trend as broken based on this pullback alone. A stock can remain in a larger bullish trend while experiencing significant short-term weakness.

Bullish scenario

The first step would be stabilization and a reclaim of $932.64. Simply touching it isn't enough. I'd want to see MU get back above it and prove it can hold as support. Additional resistance in the upper-$930s and around the short-term moving averages would still need to be worked through.

Bearish scenario

Failure to reclaim $932.64, particularly if rallies continue getting rejected underneath it, keeps the lower zones in play. The first major area is $921–$917. If that fails, $908–$910 becomes the next area that really interests me.

Dagger Trading Take

Watch the reaction, not just the level

MU has gone from strong upside momentum to a sharp short-term pullback very quickly. Rather than trying to call the exact bottom, I'm watching how price behaves around a handful of important areas.

$932.64 is the immediate battleground. Below that, $921–$917 is an important support zone. If weakness continues, $908–$910 could become one of the more meaningful technical tests of the pullback.

The levels themselves aren't signals to buy or sell. The reaction around them is what matters.

Wait for confirmation, not assumption.
