MU: Where Does Micron Find Support After the Sharp Pullback?

Published August 19, 2026

30-Second Summary

What matters most

  • MU fell 7% Tuesday after a strong five-day run.
  • $932.64 is the first key level I'm watching.
  • If weakness continues, $921–$917 is the next important support area.
  • Below that, $908–$910 could become a bigger test for buyers.

What happened?

Micron closed Tuesday at $941.10, down 7%, ending a five-day winning streak that had pushed the stock above $1,000 on Monday.

The selloff wasn't just about MU. Chip and AI-related stocks were weak across the market. Rising Treasury yields added pressure, while investors also took profits after a strong run in many technology names.

Why MU is still interesting

Short-term momentum has weakened, but the bigger memory and AI story hasn't disappeared. Demand for memory products remains strong, which creates an interesting setup: strong longer-term demand, but a stock that is currently pulling back.

Instead of trying to guess where MU will bottom, I'm watching how it reacts around a few important price areas.

Three areas I'm watching

First Key Level $932.64 This level was important during Tuesday's trading and continued getting reactions into extended hours. If MU gets back above it and holds, that would be a positive first step.
Key Support $921–$917 If MU stays below $932.64, this is the next area where I'll be watching to see if buyers step in.
Deeper Support $908–$910 If selling continues, this is another important area that could become a bigger test for buyers.

What would improve the chart?

The first thing I'd like to see is MU move back above $932.64 and hold that area as support. That wouldn't automatically mean the pullback is over, but it would be an encouraging sign that buyers are starting to regain control.

What would concern me?

If MU keeps getting rejected below $932.64, I'd continue watching the lower support areas. A break below $921–$917 would put more attention on $908–$910.

Dagger Trading Take

Watch the reaction

MU has gone from strong momentum to a sharp pullback very quickly. I'm not trying to predict the exact bottom.

$932.64 is the first level I'm watching. Below that, $921–$917 and then $908–$910 are the areas that stand out.

The important part isn't simply reaching a level. It's how the stock reacts when it gets there.

Wait for confirmation, not assumption.
