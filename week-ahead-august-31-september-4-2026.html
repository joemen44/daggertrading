Week Ahead: August 31-September 4, 2026

Published August 30, 2026 • Jobs report, Broadcom earnings, SPY & QQQ

30-Second Setup

What matters this week

  • Friday's August jobs report is the main macro event. It could have a major impact on expectations for the Fed's September rate decision.
  • Labor data starts earlier with JOLTS Tuesday and ADP Wednesday.
  • Broadcom reports Wednesday after the close, giving the AI and semiconductor trade another major test after Nvidia.
  • Dell, Palo Alto Networks, Snowflake, HPE and Lululemon are also among the notable earnings reports.
  • Focus: jobs, interest-rate expectations and whether tech can hold last week's momentum.

Market Setup: SPY
