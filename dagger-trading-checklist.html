Quick Reference

☑️ Dagger Trading Checklist

Keep the essentials in front of you before a trade. This checklist is a quick reference for preparation and execution. It does not score your setup, check your mindset or decide whether you should take the trade.

1. Market & Setup

Know what you're trading and where price is.

2. Trade Plan

Define the trade before capital goes in.

3. Execution

Make sure the plan still makes sense when it's time to act.

4. Final Reminder

A checklist helps you remember the plan. It does not make the decision for you.
