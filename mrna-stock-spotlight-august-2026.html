MRNA: Cancer Vaccine Breakthrough Sends Shares Soaring

Published August 19, 2026

30-Second Summary

What matters most

  • MRNA closed at $174.38, up 177% after Moderna and Merck reported positive Phase 3 results for a personalized mRNA cancer treatment.
  • Trading volume reached 187.3 million shares versus a 50-day average of just 7.3 million.
  • The results mark the first successful late-stage trial of a personalized mRNA cancer therapy.
  • William Blair upgraded Moderna from Market Perform to Outperform following the news.
  • $193.61 is the key upside level I'm watching. A break above it, followed by a strong hold and continued momentum, could open the door for another move higher.

What happened?

Moderna exploded higher Wednesday after the company and Merck announced positive topline results from the Phase 3 INTerpath-001 trial.

The study tested Moderna's personalized mRNA cancer treatment, intismeran autogene, in combination with Merck's Keytruda in patients with high-risk melanoma following surgery.

The study met its main goal of extending the time before the cancer returned compared with Keytruda alone. It also met a secondary goal of preventing the disease from spreading to other organs.

The market reaction was massive. MRNA finished Wednesday at $174.38, up 177%, with approximately 187.3 million shares traded — more than 25 times its 50-day average. The move also pushed Moderna to a new 52-week high.

After-hours update: MRNA continued higher after the regular session, reaching approximately $194.88 and pushing above the key $193.61 resistance area. The move is encouraging, but after-hours trading is not confirmation by itself. The next question is whether MRNA can hold above that area when regular trading resumes.

Why MRNA is interesting now

The Phase 3 success could represent an important turning point for Moderna as the company looks for new sources of growth beyond its COVID-19 vaccine business.

Earlier trial results showed that intismeran combined with Keytruda reduced the risk of melanoma recurrence or death by 49% compared with Keytruda alone. The successful Phase 3 study now provides stronger validation of the personalized mRNA approach.

Moderna hopes the melanoma treatment could potentially reach the market in 2027, although regulatory approval is still required. More detailed Phase 3 results are expected to be presented later this year.

The bigger opportunity

Melanoma may only be the beginning. Moderna and Merck's INTerpath clinical program includes nine Phase 2 and Phase 3 trials covering melanoma, non-small cell lung cancer, bladder cancer and renal cell carcinoma.

Earlier-stage studies are also examining the technology in lung, gastric and pancreatic cancers.

Success in melanoma doesn't guarantee success in other cancers, but the result gives the technology important late-stage validation while a broader cancer pipeline is already under development.

Wall Street reaction

William Blair analyst Myles Minter upgraded Moderna from Market Perform to Outperform following the Phase 3 announcement.

Merck also gained approximately 13% Wednesday, showing that investors viewed the results as meaningful for both companies.

Two areas I'm watching

Near-Term Level $184.63 If MRNA pulls back, the reaction around this area could help show whether buyers are still willing to step in or whether the stock needs a larger reset.
Key Resistance $193.61 This is the most important level I'm watching for additional upside. A break above it, followed by a strong hold and continued momentum, could open the door for another move higher.

What would improve the chart?

MRNA has already pushed above $193.61 in after-hours trading, reaching approximately $194.88. Now I'd like to see whether it can hold that area as support and continue showing strong momentum and volume during regular trading.

If that happens, the stock could have room for another leg higher toward the next major resistance area from its longer-term decline.

What would concern me?

After a 177% one-day move, MRNA is extremely extended and volatility is likely to remain high.

If $193.61 continues to act as strong resistance and momentum begins to weaken, a significant retracement would not be surprising. In that scenario, $184.63 becomes an important area to watch for buyers.

Dagger Trading Take

$193.61 is the key level

I don't just want to see MRNA trade above it. I'm watching whether the stock can break it, hold it, and continue showing strong momentum.

If it can, further upside remains in play. If it can't, I'll be watching for a pullback and where buyers begin stepping back in.

Momentum is the key. Stay sharp.
