Simple tools for better decisions.

Practical checklists, reminders, and educational material.

Pre-Trade Checklist

  • What is the setup?
  • Where is invalidation?
  • Is momentum supporting it?
  • Is size reasonable?

During the Trade

  • Do not move the stop because of hope.
  • Do not add just because price moved against you.
  • Respect the plan.
  • Avoid staring only at P&L.

After the Trade

  • Was it planned or emotional?
  • Did you follow risk rules?
  • What would you repeat?
  • What needs to change?
