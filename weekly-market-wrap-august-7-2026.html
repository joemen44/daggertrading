Weekly Market Wrap

August 3-7, 2026

Jobs data, Fed expectations, AI earnings, SPCX lockup volatility, and the stocks that mattered most.

30-Second Summary

What mattered this week

  • Friday's July jobs report became the week's biggest macro catalyst and shifted expectations around the Federal Reserve.
  • Major indexes stayed near record territory as traders balanced cooling employment data against inflation concerns.
  • AI remained a major earnings theme, but strong numbers did not guarantee positive stock reactions.
  • SPCX faced its first major lockup expiration and found buyers around the $109 area.
  • PLTR, AMD, LLY, and SPCX were among the week's most closely watched individual names.

Wall Street closed another eventful week with major earnings reactions, shifting Federal Reserve expectations, and continued volatility across AI-related stocks. Friday's employment report became the dominant macro catalyst, while individual names continued to remind traders that the market reaction matters just as much as the headline.

📊 Market Overview

U.S. stocks entered August with strength and major indexes remained near record territory. Technology continued to be an important driver, although earnings reactions became increasingly selective.

Friday's employment report changed the tone of the week. The U.S. economy unexpectedly lost jobs in July, while prior employment growth was also revised lower. The softer labor picture pushed traders to reconsider how aggressively the Federal Reserve can remain focused on inflation if employment continues to cool.

The result was a familiar market dynamic: weaker economic data helped equities because investors interpreted it as reducing the need for additional rate increases.

🔥 Stocks in Focus

PLTR

Palantir: AI demand remained strong

Palantir delivered another strong earnings report and raised its full-year revenue outlook as demand remained firm across government and commercial AI customers. Shares responded positively.

Key takeaway: AI spending remains powerful when companies can show clear revenue growth and real-world customer demand.

AMD

Advanced Micro Devices: strong numbers, tougher expectations

AMD reported strong revenue growth and continued expansion in its data-center business, but the stock fell after earnings. The reaction highlighted how much optimism had already been priced into the shares.

Key takeaway: In a high-expectation market, beating estimates is not always enough. The market trades the difference between expectations and reality.

SPCX

SpaceX: earnings, spending, and the first major lockup

SPCX had one of its most important weeks since going public. After its first quarterly report as a public company, attention quickly shifted toward the August 6 lockup expiration. Roughly 911.5 million shares became eligible for trading, but buyers stepped in around the $109 area and the stock stabilized.

Key takeaway: Eligible to sell does not mean every shareholder sells immediately. Price action shows whether demand can absorb the added supply.

LLY

Eli Lilly: obesity and diabetes demand stayed powerful

Eli Lilly remained a major healthcare story as demand for Mounjaro and Zepbound continued to drive growth. The company also raised its 2026 revenue outlook.

Key takeaway: The GLP-1 market remains one of healthcare's strongest long-term growth themes.

🏦 The Fed Question Changed Again

Earlier in the week, services data and elevated input costs kept inflation concerns alive. Then Friday's weak employment report forced traders to weigh a different risk: tightening too much while the labor market is cooling.

That leaves the Fed balancing two competing signals — inflation remains a concern, but employment appears to be losing momentum. Upcoming inflation reports will therefore carry even more weight.

🚀 SPCX Technical Follow-Up

DaggerTrading's recent SPCX analysis focused on the August lockup expiration and the importance of watching price action instead of assuming the added share supply would automatically trigger another collapse.

Buyers defended the $109.20 area, turning it into an important short-term reference level. The next question is whether SPCX can continue building support and reclaim higher resistance levels, or whether additional supply eventually pressures the stock again.

DaggerTrading Takeaway

The reaction matters more than the headline

AMD delivered strong numbers and fell. Palantir delivered strong numbers and rallied. SPCX faced a major lockup event and still found buyers near its lows. Weak employment data helped stocks because traders viewed it through the lens of Federal Reserve policy.

Don't assume how the market should react. Watch how it actually reacts.
