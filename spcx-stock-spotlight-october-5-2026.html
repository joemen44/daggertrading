SPCX Breaks Out: What’s Driving the Move & the Levels That Matter Next

Published October 5, 2026

New Spotlight • October 5, 2026

SPCX surges as Wall Street turns more bullish and Starship stays in focus

30-SECOND SUMMARY

What matters most

  • SPCX jumped 7.6% Monday to close at $171.09, extending its two-session gain to nearly 16%.
  • Fresh bullish analyst commentary helped support the move. Morgan Stanley maintained an Overweight rating and $300 price target, while arguing the shares still look attractive relative to expected growth.
  • A SpaceX unit also proposed a 32.4-mile natural-gas pipeline in Florida to support Starship launch operations at Cape Canaveral as activity expands.
  • The rally pushed through the larger $167.34 structural level. The $167.34–$167.50 area is now the key bull-bear pivot if SPCX gives back part of the move.
  • Above price, $173.04–$173.25 is the first upside resistance area, followed by $175.23, $179.29 and $181.75–$182.09. Below the key pivot, the downside roadmap opens toward $165.96, $164.10–$164.79 and lower support.

What happened?

SPCX surged 7.6% Monday and closed at $171.09, extending a sharp two-session advance to nearly 16%. The move carried the stock through several levels from the prior Dagger Trading roadmap, including the larger $167.34 area that had been an important upside checkpoint.

There was not one obvious catalyst behind the rally. Instead, the move came alongside fresh bullish Wall Street commentary, supportive research across the space theme and another sign that SpaceX is building infrastructure for a higher Starship launch cadence. After a move this fast, the focus shifts from explaining every dollar of the rally to watching whether price can hold the levels it just reclaimed.

Latest News

Starship infrastructure expands in Florida: SpaceX unit Coastal Connect Services proposed a 32.4-mile, 16-inch natural-gas transmission pipeline in Brevard County. The proposed line would connect with the Florida Gas Transmission system and supply fuel for SpaceX rocket operations at Cape Canaveral, including Starship launches, as operations grow.

Starship remains central to the growth story: Recent analyst commentary continues to focus on faster Starship launch cadence and greater reusability as major potential value drivers. That makes upcoming Starship execution important to both the operating story and investor expectations.

Momentum has accelerated: Monday's 7.6% gain followed another strong session Friday, leaving SPCX up nearly 16% across two trading days and back near its highest levels since June.

Wall Street Reaction

Morgan Stanley analyst Adam Jonas maintained an Overweight rating and $300 price target on SPCX. His argument is not that the shares look cheap on traditional trailing measures. Instead, Morgan Stanley believes the valuation becomes more attractive when adjusted for the company's expected growth, calling the stock “cheap and getting cheaper” in its research.

The bullish tone extends beyond one firm. Recent supportive research has highlighted SPCX as a way to participate in the growing space economy, with faster Starship launches and broader sector growth among the themes analysts are watching. Recent coverage also noted new Buy ratings from TD Cowen and CLSA.

Key Risks to Watch

The biggest near-term risk is simply how far and how quickly SPCX has moved. Strong momentum can continue, but a fast two-day rally also raises the risk of profit-taking and sharp intraday reversals. Longer term, much of the bullish case depends on SpaceX executing an ambitious Starship launch and reusability roadmap. Delays, launch setbacks or a slower-than-expected cadence could pressure expectations.

Valuation is another reason to stay disciplined. Bullish analysts see substantial long-term upside, but their targets are not guarantees. Price still has to confirm the story.

Updated Key Levels I'm Watching

SPCX has moved quickly, so the roadmap is intentionally layered. Start with the levels nearest price. The larger structural levels matter most if momentum begins to fade.

Key Structural Pivot$167.34–$167.50This is the most important near-term bull-bear area on the chart. The larger $167.34 level was reclaimed during Monday's breakout. Holding above this area keeps the recent breakout structure intact. If it breaks and then begins holding as resistance, the lower support roadmap becomes much more important.

The Roadmap

Upside: The latest extended-hours high near $172.88 remains a useful reference, but the first defined resistance is the tight $173.04–$173.25 area. Above that, $175.23 is the next resistance, followed by $179.29 and the larger $181.75–$182.09 resistance area. $186.12 is a farther-out roadmap level if momentum becomes unusually strong.

Downside: $171.21 is the first nearby support, followed by the $169.10–$170.38 near-term support area. The bigger level is $167.34–$167.50, the key structural pivot, with $167.34 carrying the most weight. If SPCX loses that area and begins holding below it, watch $164.10–$165.96, $161.10 and $158.13–$159.95. Farther down, $151.59–$152.59 remains the major larger-structure support area, with $152.59 especially important because it repeatedly held before the current advance.

What matters next?

The first question is whether SPCX can hold the breakout rather than simply extend it. Buyers do not need to push the stock straight higher for the structure to remain constructive. Holding reclaimed support, particularly the $167.34–$167.50 area, would matter more than chasing every new high.

Beyond the chart, watch for additional analyst revisions, Starship launch and reusability progress, and further evidence that SpaceX is expanding the infrastructure needed for a higher launch cadence.

Dagger Trading Take

The story is getting stronger. The chart still has to confirm it.

SPCX now has several bullish narratives working at once: fresh analyst support, ambitious Starship expectations and expanding launch infrastructure. Monday's breakout shows buyers are paying attention.

But after a nearly 16% two-session move, chasing the story is not the same as trading the setup. The $167.34–$167.50 area is the level I care about most if SPCX starts pulling back. As long as the breakout structure holds, the higher roadmap stays in play. If that area breaks and turns into resistance, the downside roadmap deserves much more attention.

Momentum is key. Stay sharp.
