Week Ahead: October 5-9, 2026

Published October 5, 2026 • ISM services, Fed minutes, jobless claims, consumer sentiment, PEP & DAL earnings

30-Second Setup

What matters this week

  • The rate conversation changed after Friday's weak jobs report. September payrolls rose by just 29,000, sharply reducing expectations for another Fed hike in October.
  • Monday's ISM Services report is the first major test. Traders will watch the headline, employment and prices components for clues on growth and inflation.
  • Wednesday's Fed minutes are the week's main policy event. The minutes from the September meeting could show how officials viewed inflation, labor conditions and the need for additional rate increases.
  • SPY and QQQ finished Friday strong. Both ETFs closed near the upper end of the week's range, making Friday's highs the first simple upside reference while the week's lows remain important support.
  • Earnings are lighter before the big banks arrive next week. Constellation Brands, Levi Strauss, PepsiCo and Delta Air Lines are among the recognizable names reporting.
  • Dagger Trading focus: The calendar is lighter than last week, but rates can still move the market. Know the levels and let price confirm the reaction.

Market Setup: SPY

SPY closed Friday at $769.64, rebounding into the end of the week after softer employment data reduced expectations for an October Fed rate hike. The broader setup remains close to recent highs, but Treasury yields and the Fed outlook are still capable of changing momentum quickly.

Friday High / Near-Term Resistance$772.65A clean break and hold above Friday's high would keep buyers pressing higher and make it the first upside reference heading into the week.
Friday Low / First Support$767.15This is the first nearby reference if Friday's rebound starts to fade.
Last Week's High$772.65Friday also set the high for the week, making this the clearest near-term breakout level.
Last Week's Low$758.79Losing the week's low would represent a more meaningful deterioration in the short-term structure.

SPY takeaway: Friday's high is the first upside test. If buyers cannot hold above it, watch whether $767.15 and then the broader weekly range continue to support the move.

QQQ: Tech Finishes the Week Strong

QQQ closed Friday at $749.58 after reaching a weekly high of $754.54. Technology outperformed the broader market for the week, but the same rate sensitivity remains in play: softer data can support growth stocks through lower rate expectations, while renewed inflation or yield pressure can quickly challenge momentum.

Friday High / Near-Term Resistance$754.54This is the first upside test and also the high for the entire week.
Friday Low / First Support$747.53Holding this area would keep Friday's stronger short-term structure intact.
Last Week's High$754.54A sustained move above the weekly high would signal that tech leadership is extending.
Last Week's Low$731.63A move back toward the weekly low would represent a much larger loss of momentum.

Economic Calendar: Fed Minutes & Services Data

After last week's labor-heavy calendar, this week shifts toward services activity, the Fed's September meeting minutes and another read on consumer confidence. The key question is whether incoming data reinforces the market's reduced expectations for an October rate hike.

DateEventWhy Traders Care
Mon. Oct. 5ISM Services PMI — 10:00 AM ETThe services sector represents a large share of U.S. activity. Traders will watch growth, employment and prices for signals that could move Treasury yields and rate expectations.

Key Earnings This Week

Earnings are relatively light before the major banks begin reporting next week, but several recognizable companies can still create stock-specific volatility.

Tuesday — STZConstellation Brands reports after the close. Demand trends, margins and management's outlook will be closely watched.
Wednesday — LEVI & APLDLevi Strauss and Applied Digital report after the close, giving traders separate reads on consumer apparel and AI/data-center infrastructure.
Thursday — PEPPepsiCo reports before the open. Pricing, volumes and consumer demand make it one of the week's larger earnings reports.
Friday — DALDelta Air Lines reports before the open. Travel demand, pricing and costs will provide an early read on the airline sector.

Earnings to Watch

PEP — PepsiCo

Thursday before the open. PepsiCo is one of the largest companies reporting this week. Traders will be watching organic sales, pricing, volumes, margins and guidance for clues about how consumers are responding to higher prices.

DAL — Delta Air Lines

Friday before the open. Delta's report puts travel demand back in focus. Revenue trends, fares, fuel costs and management's outlook can shape the reaction across the airline group.

Dagger Trading Take

The calendar is quieter. The rate reaction still matters.

Last week's weak jobs report changed the market's expectations for the Fed, but one report does not settle the rate debate. ISM Services, Fed minutes, jobless claims and consumer sentiment all give traders another chance to reassess growth and inflation.

SPY and QQQ finished Friday with momentum. The job this week is not to assume that momentum continues. Start with Friday's highs and lows, watch Treasury yields, and let price show whether buyers can extend the move.
