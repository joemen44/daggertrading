Mindset Tool

🗡️ Dagger Check

Check yourself before you check the trade. Six quick questions to spot emotions that can interfere with your decision.

  • Check whether you're calm and thinking clearly.
  • Catch FOMO and revenge trading.
  • Recognize boredom or the urge for action.
  • Make sure you're ready to follow your rules.

Setup Tool

🗡️ Dagger Setup Score

Score the quality of the setup. Evaluate how clearly your planned trade is defined before putting capital at risk.

  • Trend and momentum.
  • Key levels and invalidation.
  • Risk/reward and entry quality.
  • Catalyst and event risk.

Execution Tool

🗡️ Should I Take This Trade?

Check the actual decision before you enter. Walk through the trade you're considering right now and catch problems before you execute.

  • Check your planned entry and stop.
  • Define a realistic target.
  • Look for confirmation instead of anticipation.
  • Catch chasing or repeated entries.
