About

Dagger Trading

Built from years of market experience, mistakes, adjustments, and a growing belief that process matters more than prediction.

Clear thinking in unpredictable markets.

I have spent more than 14 years studying markets. Along the way, I have chased, overtraded, hesitated, averaged down too early, fought momentum, and learned many lessons the expensive way.

Dagger Trading is not about pretending every setup is guaranteed or trying to call every top and bottom. The focus is on momentum, patience, confirmation, risk management, and making better decisions when emotions are running high.

The site brings together market commentary, trading psychology, educational comics, practical resources, and a live Market Center designed to keep traders informed without unnecessary hype.
