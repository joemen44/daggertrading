AVGO: AI Growth Surges as Shares Whipsaw After Earnings

Published September 2, 2026

Latest Update — September 3, 2026

The post-earnings recovery is testing a key area

What happened?

AVGO opened weak following earnings and fell to $342.20 before recovering sharply. The rebound carried shares back through the mid-$350s, putting the $353.09 area back in focus.

That area remains the key near-term level I’m watching. Holding it as support keeps the recovery attempt intact. If it breaks and begins holding as strong resistance with bearish momentum, the risk of another move lower increases.

Wall Street reaction

The long-term AI story remains strong, but expectations were extremely high heading into the report. StoneX analyst Cody Acree noted that Nvidia’s recent results raised the bar for the sector and that Broadcom’s fourth-quarter outlook implied earnings per share below Wall Street expectations.

That helps explain the disconnect between Broadcom’s aggressive 2027 and 2028 AI outlook and the weak initial stock reaction. Investors may want to see more evidence of execution before fully pricing those longer-term forecasts into the shares.

Key Support$353.09 areaHolding this area as support keeps the recovery attempt intact. A break that turns it into strong resistance would increase downside risk.
Immediate Pivot$356.60AVGO has repeatedly interacted with this area. Holding above it as support strengthens the recovery, while rejection and a move back below puts $353.09 back in focus.
Next Important Test~$361 areaIf $356.60 is reclaimed and held with improving momentum, this becomes the next important upside test.
Larger Upside Target$381.26This remains the larger upside objective from the main daily setup if AVGO can continue rebuilding momentum through the levels below it.

Dagger Trading Take

$353.09 first. Then let the recovery prove itself.

AVGO has recovered sharply from the morning low, but the chart still needs confirmation. The first job is holding the $353.09 area. Above that, $356.60 is the immediate pivot. If buyers can reclaim and hold it with momentum, the ~$361 area becomes the next important test, with $381.26 still the larger upside target.

Momentum is key.

Broadcom • Fiscal Q3 2026

Original Spotlight — September 2, 2026

Published after earnings

30-SECOND SUMMARY

What matters most

  • Broadcom reported $29.59 billion in revenue, up 86% year over year and above expectations.
  • Adjusted EPS came in at $3.32, above the roughly $3.22 FactSet estimate.
  • AI semiconductor revenue reached $16.7 billion, up 221% year over year.
  • AVGO initially plunged to $342.20 after hours before recovering sharply.
  • $361.07 is the immediate support level I’m watching. If buyers can hold it and regain momentum, $381.26 becomes the major upside area.

Strong quarter. Complicated reaction.

Broadcom delivered another strong quarter, but the first reaction after earnings was anything but simple. Shares initially plunged to $342.20 after hours before recovering sharply as investors digested the results and management’s longer-term AI outlook.

What happened?

Revenue reached $29.59 billion, up 86% from a year earlier, while adjusted earnings came in at $3.32 per share. Both figures were above Wall Street expectations.

Semiconductor-solutions revenue reached roughly $20.8 billion, while AI semiconductor revenue climbed to $16.7 billion. Broadcom said demand for custom AI accelerators and networking remains very strong.

The AI story keeps getting bigger

Broadcom expects AI semiconductor revenue to reach approximately $21.7 billion in Q4. Management also said it has secured enough supply to support roughly $115 billion of AI semiconductor revenue in fiscal 2027, with demand exceeding that outlook.

Looking further ahead, CEO Hock Tan said Broadcom has line of sight toward approximately $230 billion of AI semiconductor revenue in fiscal 2028 and expects the company to exceed $30 in EPS that year.

The custom-chip business is becoming increasingly important. Management discussed work involving Google, OpenAI, Meta and Anthropic, with Anthropic expected to become Broadcom’s largest custom-chip customer in 2027.

Why did AVGO sell off?

The initial drop did not come from an outright earnings miss. Broadcom beat revenue and adjusted EPS expectations, and its Q4 revenue outlook of roughly $34.8 billion was slightly above consensus.

The issue was expectations. With enthusiasm around Broadcom’s AI business already extremely high, the near-term outlook offered only limited upside versus Wall Street estimates. Investors were also weighing the effect of a growing custom-silicon mix on gross margins and competition across the AI-chip market.

As the earnings call progressed and management detailed its much larger 2027 and 2028 AI opportunity, AVGO recovered a large portion of the initial selloff.

Post-earnings price action

AVGO initially fell as low as $342.20 after earnings before buyers stepped in aggressively. That low marks the extreme of the immediate post-earnings reaction and serves as the lower anchor for the current retracement structure I’m watching.

The recovery was impressive, but that does not automatically make the setup bullish. Price still needs to prove that buyers can defend support and continue reclaiming higher levels.

Key levels I’m watching

Immediate Support $361.07 This is the first decision level. Holding it keeps the post-earnings recovery structure intact.
Major Upside Area $381.26 If $361.07 holds and buyers regain momentum, this becomes the major upside area I’m watching.
Key Support $353.09 If $361.07 fails, this becomes the next important support area to watch.
Deeper Support Zone $328.95–$321.50 If $353.09 breaks and begins holding as resistance, this former support zone becomes increasingly important.

Post-earnings low: $342.20 remains an important reference point from the earnings reaction, but I’m keeping the active roadmap focused on the levels above.

Dagger Trading Take

Strong numbers. Let price confirm the next move.

Broadcom’s earnings and longer-term AI outlook were impressive, but strong fundamentals do not guarantee an immediate move higher. After a reaction this volatile, I would rather let price confirm the next direction than chase the headline.

For now, $361.07 is the line in the sand. Hold it and $381.26 becomes the major upside area. Lose it and $353.09 becomes the next support test. If that breaks, the deeper $328.95–$321.50 zone comes into play.

Wait for confirmation, not assumption.
