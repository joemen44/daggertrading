Before You Trade

🗡️ The Dagger Trading Decision Check

Six quick questions to test your process before entering a trade. It does not tell you what to buy or sell—it helps you define the setup, risk, and whether emotion is getting involved.

🧠 Process firstJudge the decision—not the eventual P&L.
🛡️ Risk definedKnow where the trade is wrong before capital goes in.
⏳ No rushMissing a trade is cheaper than forcing one.

1. Is there a clear setup?

Can you explain what you are trading in one sentence?

2. Is momentum confirming the trade?

Is price action, volume, trend, or another part of your strategy supporting the direction?

3. Do you know where you'll exit if the trade goes against you?

Do you have a clear stop or price level that tells you the setup is no longer working—and will you respect it?

4. Is the risk/reward acceptable?

Does realistic upside justify the planned downside?

5. Are you chasing?

Would you still want the trade if you had not just watched it make a fast move without you?

6. Is emotion driving the decision?

Are boredom, FOMO, frustration, revenge, or the urge to make back a loss pushing the trade?

🗡️ Dagger Rule: A trade can lose money and still be a good decision if you followed your process. A trade can make money and still be a bad decision if you ignored your rules. Wait for confirmation, not assumption.

The Dagger Trading Decision Check is an educational self-assessment tool only. It does not evaluate a security, predict outcomes, or provide financial advice. Trading and investing involve risk, including possible loss of principal.
