Before You Trade

🗡️ Dagger Check

Check yourself before you check the trade.

Six quick questions to check your mindset before an intraday or swing trade.

🧠 Check yourselfYour state of mind can affect the trade before it even starts.
🎯 Follow your rulesA good opportunity does not require you to abandon your process.
⏳ No rushMissing a trade is cheaper than forcing one.

1. Are you calm enough to make a clear decision?

Are you thinking clearly, or are you feeling rushed, frustrated, overly excited, or tense?

Beginner tip: Strong emotions can make a trade feel more urgent or obvious than it really is.

2. Are you trading because you’re afraid of missing out?

Would you still want to trade if you had not just watched price make a fast move?

Beginner tip: FOMO can turn “I see an opportunity” into “I need to get in now.”

3. Are you trying to make back a loss?

Is your last losing trade influencing how badly you want this one?

Beginner tip: Revenge trading happens when recovering money becomes more important than waiting for a good decision.

4. Are you trading because you’re bored or just want action?

If nothing interesting were happening, would you be comfortable doing nothing?

Beginner tip: Wanting to stay busy can make an average trade suddenly look good enough.

5. Can you accept the loss before you take the trade?

If this trade loses within your plan, can you accept it without immediately trying to win the money back?

Beginner tip: If the possible loss already feels emotionally difficult, your size or mindset may need another look.

6. Are you willing to follow your rules on this trade?

Will you respect your plan even if price starts moving quickly or your emotions change?

Beginner tip: A trading rule only protects you if you are willing to follow it when the trade gets uncomfortable.
